# Prompt Log — Daftar

Two parts. **Part A** documents the product planning and engineering decisions made in Claude chat before implementation. **Part B** records significant implementation prompts in Claude Code, including model and effort where specified.

The log captures the reasoning behind the work: the constraints established, alternatives evaluated, decisions made, quality standards defined, and implementation progress reviewed.

## Logging conventions

- **Prompt:** Capture the engineering intent and constraints, not necessarily the verbatim wording used in chat.
- **Reason:** Explain the problem or risk that motivated the instruction.
- **Outcome:** Record what actually happened, not what the prompt intended to achieve.
- **Model / effort:** Record only explicitly stated values. Use “Not stated” where the original prompt did not include them.
- **Decision ownership:** Distinguish decisions made by me from alternatives proposed by the AI and subsequently accepted.
- **Mistakes:** Record observed agent errors and their corrective actions without turning the log into a retrospective of hypothetical failures.

The purpose of this log is to make the reasoning and decision-making behind Daftar visible and reviewable, including how AI assistance was directed, constrained, and validated throughout the project.
